Apostear

Equity & Inclusion · Digital

Closing the digital divide in Rosario's most vulnerable neighborhoods

What we do

We repair unused computers, set them up in community spaces, and run weekly digital skills workshops — from basic computer use to responsible AI — in neighborhoods where access to technology has never been a given.

Right now

  • Weekly workshops in 4 neighborhoods: República de la Sexta, San Francisquito, Tío Rolo, and Zona Norte
  • Teaching G-Suite, Canva, CapCut, digital wallets, and intro to AI
  • Partnered with the Government of Santa Fe and 3 civil society orgs
  • Looking to expand to more neighborhoods and connect students with scholarships

¿Y Ahora Qué?

Future of Work · Education

Helping high schoolers navigate an uncertain future — without pretending we have all the answers

What we do

Participatory workshops for students in their final years of high school. We use Ikigai-based dynamics, peer reflection, and honest conversations about a job market where 80% of future roles don't exist yet.

Right now

  • Running sessions across public and private schools in Rosario
  • Covering entrepreneurship, networking, soft skills, and AI in the workplace
  • Scaling the 2026 edition with a team of 11 volunteers
  • Looking for companies to co-design sessions grounded in real hiring needs

Aire Global

Climate · Open Data · Advocacy

Real-time air quality data for a city that's been breathing in the dark

What we do

Since 2020, intentional fires in the Paraná wetlands have been polluting Rosario's air — with no public data available. We installed 12 air quality sensors across the city and are now making that data public, educating communities, and turning evidence into policy pressure.

Right now

  • 12 sensors capturing real-time air quality data across strategic points in the city
  • Processing and visualizing data for public access
  • Running community education on air quality and health impacts
  • Building the evidence base to push for concrete policy action
Let's work together if…

Luna Roja

Health · Gender · Sustainability

Accessible, sustainable menstrual health for vulnerable communities in Rosario

What we do

Participatory workshops on menstrual health and comprehensive sex education in Rosario's most underserved neighborhoods — using a game-based dynamic that creates real, honest dialogue. Every session ends with the distribution of menstrual cups as a sustainable alternative.

Right now

  • Running workshops with volunteer medical students as co-facilitators
  • Expanded to include all genders — making sex education more inclusive
  • Reaching schools and community spaces across Rosario
  • Looking to grow our network of health professionals and partner organizations
